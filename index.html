About the Group

A geometric path into curved spacetime

This study group gives students a structured entry into General Relativity, building from the ground up: smooth manifolds, tangent spaces, and bundles first; then connections and parallel transport; then curvature, spacetime structure, and finally the Einstein equation. Every step emphasizes both the precise geometric language and the physical intuition it encodes.

Alongside the main lecture series, the group hosts weekly invited talks on topics at the frontier of mathematical physics — from quantum foundations and Bohmian mechanics to gauge/gravity duality and the deep nature of space and time.
